How Spotify Sync works: Register a collaborative Spotify playlist and link it to a style. Each night at 3am UTC, the sync fetches the current playlist, matches tracks against the library, and automatically adds or removes songs from the style to mirror the playlist. Songs in the playlist that don't exist in the library are flagged as unmatched — click the unmatched count on any row to see the full list and export it as CSV for the curation team to source.
